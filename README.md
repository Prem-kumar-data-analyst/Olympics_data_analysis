# 🏅 Olympics Data Analysis Dashboard

![Dashboard](Images/dashboard.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes historical Olympic Games data using Python, Pandas, and Power BI to uncover trends in athlete participation, medals, sports performance, and demographic characteristics.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into Olympic history, helping users understand participation growth, medal distribution, and athlete characteristics.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### ✅ Male vs Female Participation Over Years
Analyzes how participation of male and female athletes has changed over time.

### ✅ Weight vs Height Correlation
Scatter plot showing the relationship between athletes' height and weight.

### ✅ Athlete Participation by Year & Season
Comparison of Summer and Winter Olympics participation.

### ✅ Top 10 Sports by Total Medals
Sports with the highest number of medals.

### ✅ Top 10 Countries by Total Medals
Countries with the most Olympic medals.

### ✅ Age Distribution of Winners
Distribution of medal winners based on age.

### ✅ Weight Distribution of Winners
Weight distribution among medal-winning athletes.

### ✅ Height Distribution of Winners
Height distribution among medal-winning athletes.

# 📊 Key Insights

- Male participation dominated earlier Olympics, while female participation has steadily increased.
- Summer Olympics consistently have more athletes than Winter Olympics.
- Athletics has won the highest number of medals.
- USA leads the overall Olympic medal tally.
- Most medal winners fall between 20–30 years of age.
- Most medal-winning athletes have heights between 170–185 cm.
- Most medal-winning athletes weigh between 60–90 kg.
